# Cold-Tossed Dishes

## What Is Cold Tossing?

Cold tossing is a way to prepare a dish by mixing main ingredients with accompanying ingredients

### Forms of Cold-Tossed Dishes

The main ingredients and accompanying ingredients can be mixed together throughout a cold-tossed dish
The main ingredients and accompanying ingredients can also be kept separate; in this case, the accompanying ingredients are called a dipping sauce

### Why Make Cold-Tossed Dishes?

* Some cold-tossed dishes do not require a heat source to prepare
* Some cold-tossed dishes reduce the need to wash a pot or pan (no washing, or just a rinse)
* Cold tossing can preserve ingredients in their original state, especially vegetables and raw meat

### The Purpose of Cold Tossing

* The purpose of cold tossing is to add flavor to bland or mildly flavored ingredients, such as chicken ribs

### What Can Go into a Cold-Tossed Dish?

Including, but not limited to:

* Main ingredients
* Accompanying ingredients
* Marinade sauces
* Seasonings

### Things to Keep in Mind

* Pay attention to ingredient safety when making cold-tossed dishes. If you are unsure whether an ingredient is safe, do not use it in a cold-tossed dish; if you know an ingredient is unsafe, you should not use it in a cold-tossed dish
* Maximize the exposed surface area of the main ingredients, so scoring, slicing, smashing, or even crushing them is recommended
* Cold-tossed dishes make certain basic demands on your digestive system. Choose this method only once you are sure you will not have explosive diarrhea, or you have time set aside for it
* This document was written during the COVID pandemic. For peace of mind, it is recommended that all ingredients be heated in an environment above 100 degrees Celsius for more than 15 seconds. If you want to be safe, avoid cold-tossed dishes as much as possible

## Equipment

You can use any container, from a ceramic crock to a food-grade plastic bag

### Things to Keep in Mind

* To keep ingredients from spilling out while mixing, a rigid container with more than twice the volume of all the ingredients is a good choice
* For food safety, after marinating ingredients in a plastic bag or plastic bowl, transfer them to a ceramic or metal container as soon as possible
* For food safety, prepare ingredients and accompanying ingredients that will be eaten raw on a clean cutting board

## Procedure

### Preparing Leafy or Layered Vegetables as Main Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

Examples: cabbage, lettuce, tender napa cabbage hearts, onions, etc.

* Tear the ingredients into small pieces measuring 4cm * 4cm
* Cut the ingredients into strips 0.5cm long
* Trim off the unused parts and use the leaves or layers whole
* Blanch the prepared ingredients

#### Things to Keep in Mind

Some ingredients do not need to be separated into leaves or layers. Simply cut along their lengthwise axis, then cut crosswise into sections. If you are confident in your knife skills, you can cut directly across the axis into sections

### Preparing Chunky Vegetables as Main Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

Examples: potatoes, water chestnuts, cucumbers, spuds, etc.

* Cut the ingredients into long strips with a cross section of 0.5cm * 0.5cm
* Cut the ingredients into 4cm * 4cm slices less than 0.5cm thick
* Smash or crush the ingredients with the flat of a knife (especially suitable for cucumbers)
* Use the ingredients as they are (especially suitable for ingredients that already come in small pieces)
* Blanch the prepared ingredients

#### Things to Keep in Mind

Smashing ingredients may send pieces flying. You can put them in a food-grade plastic bag before smashing them

### Preparing Chunky Fungi as Main Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

Examples: various mushrooms, various wood ear fungi, etc.

* Soak the ingredients to rehydrate them
* Cut the ingredients into long strips with a cross section of 0.5cm * 0.5cm
* Cut the ingredients into 4cm * 4cm slices less than 0.5cm thick
* Use the ingredients as they are (especially suitable for ingredients that already come in small pieces)
* Blanch the prepared ingredients

#### Things to Keep in Mind

You must be absolutely sure that any fungi are safe. Once you are laid out in a coffin, it is all over

### Preparing Pieces of Meat as Main Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

Examples: fish, jellyfish heads, cooked pork, cooked poultry, etc.

* Cook the ingredients by steaming, boiling, roasting, frying, or another method
* Soak the ingredients in cool water for a little while (especially suitable for seafood)
* Tear the ingredients into strips of meat
* Cut the ingredients into thin slices (especially suitable for boiled pork)
* Cut the ingredients into long strips with a cross section of 0.8cm * 0.8cm
* Tear the ingredients apart by body part or cut them into large pieces (especially suitable for whole cooked birds)

#### Things to Keep in Mind

* Pork and poultry must be fully cooked, without exception. They must be cooked all the way through, with no trace of bloody juices
* Some beef, fish, and seafood can be eaten raw once you have confirmed that they are safe

### Preparing Ingredients in the "I Reckon This'll Work Too" Category (Avoid This Step If Possible; All Options Required)

Examples: noodles, rice, fruit, tender tree leaves, etc.

* Confirm that the ingredients are safe
* Prepare the ingredients so they are edible
* Prepare the ingredients so they can be mixed

#### Things to Keep in Mind

* Make sure the ingredients are safe. You can crush a small amount and smear it on your upper arm for 30 minutes to test for an allergic reaction. Discard the ingredient if any abnormal reaction occurs
* Most tree leaves and grasses contain substances that can make you feel unwell, even after cooking. Make sure they are safe
* Most grains are difficult to digest and absorb before cooking and may have adverse effects on your digestive system

### Preparing Accompanying Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

Examples: bird's eye chili peppers, garlic cloves, ginger, dried chili peppers, etc.

* Remove the stems from the bird's eye chili peppers, wash them, trim off 0.5cm at the stem end, then mince them or cut them into sections
* Smash and peel the garlic cloves, trim off 0.5cm at the root end, then mince them or cut them into sections
* Peel the ginger, then mince it, julienne it, or slice it
* Crush the dried chili peppers
* Put the prepared ingredients in a small bowl or the container you will use for mixing
* Add powdered ingredients, seasonings, or marinade sauces to the container and mix thoroughly

#### Things to Keep in Mind

* There is an extremely wide range of accompanying ingredients and ways to prepare them. Do not limit your imagination, but check things carefully, use appropriate methods and amounts, and keep safety in mind

### Mixing the Ingredients (Optional Step; Choose One or More Options)

* Squeeze excess water out of meat ingredients with a high water content, then drain them
* Put vegetable ingredients with a high water content in a container, add salt at a ratio of about 200g : 5g of vegetables to salt, mix, let stand for 5 minutes, and drain off the water
* Add the main ingredients and accompanying ingredients to the mixing container
* Mix with chopsticks, a spoon, or your hands, after washing whichever you use
* Seal the container and shake until evenly mixed
* Tilt the container to an angle at which no ingredients will spill out, then rotate it around its central axis

#### Things to Keep in Mind

* Ingredients with a high water content may release too much water if added directly, diluting the seasonings
* If there is not enough liquid or the ingredients are not mixing evenly, add an appropriate amount of boiled, cooled water. If you are unsure how much to use, 15mL at a time is a good amount
* Mixing is not recommended for some highly absorbent ingredients, as they may become too strongly flavored after marinating

### Eating (Required Step)

* Eat the mixed ingredients directly
* Dip the unmixed main ingredients into the dipping sauce and eat
* Add the ingredients and dipping sauce to a staple food and eat
