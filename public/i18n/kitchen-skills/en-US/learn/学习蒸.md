# Steaming

## Methods

### Steamer Pot

A steamer pot has multiple tiers. The bottom holds water, and the heat from the steam produced when the water boils cooks the food in the tiers above.

Add an appropriate amount of water to the bottom of the steamer pot——Place the food in the steamer trays above——Place the steamer pot over the heat

### Iron Wok

If you do not have a steamer pot and only have a regular iron wok (not a flat-bottomed pan), you can place a tripod rack in the bottom and add enough water to achieve a similar effect.

Add enough water to the bottom of the wok———Put in the tripod rack———Place the food on the tripod rack———Turn on the heat and cover with the lid

## Precautions

* Because steam is the heat source, the bottoms of food in the lower steamer trays may get wet from the water. You can place a steamer cloth under the food to prevent this. You can also use chopsticks to make a simple rack to hold the food.
* You can use a smart device to set a timer to remind you to turn off the heat, so you do not forget and let the pot boil dry.
* When steaming food, watch the amount of water remaining in the bottom, **especially when using an iron wok**, to avoid the safety hazards of letting it boil dry.
* (Optional) When steaming food in an iron wok, you can place a steaming plate on top of the tripod rack.
