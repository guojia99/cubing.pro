
# Stir-Frying/Pan-Frying

## Equipment

You can use a regular metal (iron/stainless steel/aluminum) wok or frying pan, or a nonstick pan.

Aluminum cookware is not recommended; see the food safety section for details.

### Precautions

* Ways to keep food from sticking when stir-frying in a regular pan:

#### The Fry-an-Egg-First Method

* Before stir-frying any dish, fry an egg first. Do not wash the pan afterward, and the next dish you stir-fry will not stick.

#### The Hot-Pan, Cool-Oil Method

* Remember: the pan must be hot and the oil cool. First, heat the pan.
  * Heat a clean, empty pan until it is evenly heated and hot.
  * Add cool oil and swirl the pan so that the oil coats the entire pan (you can swirl it back and forth to heat it evenly).
  * When you see fumes rising from the pan, the oil is hot.
  * Pour out the oil, and do not wash the pan afterward.
  * You can repeat the steps above 2-3 times for a better nonstick effect.
  * Caution: On a gas stove, flames may flare up. Be careful.

#### The Hot-Pan, Two-Stage Oil Method

* First, heat the pan.
  * Heat a clean, empty pan until it is evenly heated and hot.
  * Add "a small amount of cool oil" and swirl the pan so that the oil coats the entire pan (you can swirl it back and forth to heat it evenly).
  * When you see fumes rising from the pan, the oil is hot.
  * Add more cool oil and start stir-frying.
  * Caution: On a gas stove, flames may flare up. Be careful.

Additional notes:

* The goal is to coat the entire bottom of the pan with oil. This works with all household pans on the market, instantly making them nonstick once coated.
* Food will not stick when pan-fried or stir-fried in a nonstick pan. A nonstick pan gets this property from the coating on its inner surface. **Metal spatulas will scratch the coating. Use a wooden or silicone spatula with a nonstick pan to avoid damaging the coating.**

### Procedure

Turn on the heat——Place the pan flat directly over the heat and let it get hot——Pour oil into the pan and let it get hot——Add the ingredients and stir-fry——Remember to add seasonings before removing the food from the pan

### Precautions

* To check whether the pan/oil is hot, hold your hand flat above the pan to feel the heat; add ingredients only after the oil is hot.
* Before pouring oil into the pan, make sure there is no water left inside. **Water will cause hot oil to splatter, creating a hazard.**
* Following the point above, drain water from ingredients before adding them to hot oil (beaten eggs are fine). Likewise, do not add ingredients that have not been thawed to hot oil, as melting ice can create a hazard.
* **If the oil in the pan catches fire, never pour water on it to put it out**. Doing so will spread the fire. When the fire first starts, you can quickly turn off the heat and cover the pan with a lid.
