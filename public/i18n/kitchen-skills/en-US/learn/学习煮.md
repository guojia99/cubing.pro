# Boiling

## Procedure

Pour water into the pot——Turn on the heat and place the pot over it——Once the water boils (it churns and produces lots of bubbles), add the ingredients

### Precautions

* Covering the pot while heating helps it heat up faster. **However, this creates a risk of boiling over**. With continued heating, excessively bubbling liquid may spill out of the pot; this is called boiling over.
* **If the pot is about to boil over, immediately turn down the heat and remove the lid.**
* To heat the contents faster while avoiding a boilover, leave the lid partly open so steam can escape. You can also turn down the heat later on and keep a close eye on the pot.
* Depending on what you are cooking, you can also add the ingredients to cold water. However, this will make the water take longer to boil.
