# How to Decide What to Eat

Deciding what to eat is also a major challenge for me before I start cooking. So the only thing to do is describe it mathematically.

## Calculation Method

### Calculating the Number of Meat and Vegetable Dishes

* Number of dishes = number of people + 1.
* Have either one more meat dish than vegetable dishes, or an equal number of each.

Once you have the numbers of meat and vegetable dishes, choose that many from the recipes in the previous step.

#### Formal Description

Given `N` people,
let `a` be the `number of vegetable dishes` and `b` be the `number of meat dishes`.
`N`, `a`, and `b` are all integers.

This gives the following system of inequalities:

* a + b = N + 1
* a ≤ b ≤ a+1

Solving gives:

```javascript
const a = Math.floor((N+1)/2);
const b = Math.ceil((N+1)/2);
```

### Choosing Dishes

* If there are more than 8 people, consider including a fish dish among the meat dishes.
* If children will be eating, consider adding a sweet dish.
* Consider adding specialty dishes or dishes you make particularly well.
* When choosing meat dishes, do not use meat from the same animal for all of them. Consider them in this order: `pork`, `chicken`, `beef`, `lamb`, `duck`, `fish`.
* Do not choose unusual animals for meat dishes.

If you still cannot decide, use the [What Should I Eat Today?](https://github.com/ryanuo/whatToEat) tool to choose what to eat today.
